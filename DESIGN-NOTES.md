# Extension, not a reskin

The requested change is a 2v2 option. The Atelier wordmark, neutral surfaces, fine separators, camera chapters and typography remain. A compact flat match switch is the only new permanent control. Extra player views and inspector tabs appear only when relevant.

Player IDs are interleaved by side for compatibility with the original Mica/Ember IDs: blue 0/2, orange 1/3. Team colours use close but distinguishable shades; labels provide explicit identity. No decorative player cards or enlarged dashboard were added.

Technical information is on demand in Fieldnotes. It separates the authored team assignment from the selected policy-scored plan. The current-mode pass/assist counts are explicitly aggregate. Replays do not masquerade as stored historical intent.

The cached Canvas arena and WebGL arena share meshes. Follow-camera cache coverage was corrected rather than reducing or hiding the stadium. Graphics fallbacks preserve the same world and policies. Source remains self-contained and asset-free.
